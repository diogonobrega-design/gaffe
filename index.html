A system that monitors water use in hotels and rewards the guests who use less of it.

Shower5:00= 50 litres
one drop at a time
Client
AQUAINN
Sector
Hospitality, water efficiency
What we did
Communication strategy, creative concept, copy, activations, video script
Format
Creative proposal, including Web Summit activations

the
question?

How do you get hotel guests to use less water without telling them off?

Hotels have to cut water in a drought. Guests use the most of it, and nobody on holiday wants a lecture.

800 LEstimated water use per hotel guest, per day

4 audiencesGuests, managers, staff and the hotel's local partners

7.5 LLost every minute a tap is left running

the
obvious
answer.

Tell people what they should or shouldn't do.

the
gaffe.

The best 5 minutes of your life?

the
insight.

What's the most impactful thing you could do in under five minutes?

  • Bungee jumping.
  • A wedding proposal.
  • Skydiving.
  • Or maybe a 5 minute shower.

Not all great experiences take long. So the campaign stops asking for a sacrifice and offers an upgrade: a short shower unlocks perks for the stay.

5 min = 50 L

The best five minutes of your life.

Based on the proposal's figures: 5 minutes uses 50 litres, 30 minutes uses 300.

what it
became.

One idea, said differently to everyone in the building.

What do we tell guests when they walk into their room?

On the toothbrush glass

“Time to brush off old ideas!”

A complimentary toothbrush with a message about the tap. Left running, it wastes around 7.5 litres a minute.

At check-in

“Put on a different kind of thinking cap!”

A shower cap nobody expects, carrying the numbers: 50 litres for five minutes, 300 for thirty.

On the room tablet

“The best 5 minutes of your life?”

A short shower is all it takes for a greener stay with extra perks.

Shower playlist

AquaSoundscapes

A Spotify playlist where every song runs under five minutes. When the track ends, so does the shower.

What do we tell managers so they partner with us?

Headline

“Help us, help you, help them!”

The pitch in one breath: the hotel, the guest and the planet all come out ahead.

Headline

“Savings beyond the water cycle!”

Less hot water also means a smaller energy bill.

The promise

Up to 30% less water. Up to 20% less heating.

The potential savings AQUAINN puts in front of every hotel.

What do we tell staff so they help us?

Training programme

HydroSustain

An internal education programme that shows hotel teams how much water runs through their everyday work, and how to manage it.

Restaurant menu

AquaArtistry

A menu built with the hotel's kitchen around water-saving practices, so the idea reaches the table too.

What do we tell local partners so they join the network?

Partner network seal

The AQUAINN seal of excellence in water usage

Awarded to the restaurants, spas, wineries, local producers and outdoor operators that meet water standards and become the rewards guests unlock.

Why they join

Guests arrive. So does business.

The network sends the hotel's guests to local businesses, so the region grows with the idea.

What do we tell Web Summit visitors so they want to know AQUAINN?

Activation

The Thinking Cap

Promoters in bathrobes and slippers hand out shower caps. One side carries the line, the other the 30% and 20% pitch.

Promo video, 20 seconds

“Water is life. And life is worth saving!”

The logo turns into a cap and lands on a head. The app does the rest.

Activation

Sustainable Unlock

A concierge walks the venue handing out keys on keychains: “Unlock a new way to manage water!”

ARA

A conceptual foundation for Comprehensive Ayurveda Healthcare. India, France, Portugal.

अर
the spoke of a wheel
Client
Comprehensive Ayurveda Healthcare
Sector
Healthcare, Ayurveda
What we did
Brand concept, naming, brand architecture, roadmap
Format
Creative proposal. See it live ↗

the
question?

Build the conceptual foundation for an Ayurveda centre in Portugal, as part of an existing system across India and France.

In India, Ayurveda is a way of understanding life. In the West it was repackaged as wellness: spa treatments with pleasure and no depth. This project lives in that gap.

the
obvious
answer.

A wellness brand. A spa concept. A collection of services.

the
gaffe.

ARA. The spoke of a wheel.

the
insight.

If health is relational, the brand must be structural.

A name applied on top would not hold. The brand had to work the way Ayurveda does: in relationships, never in parts.

अर

The spoke of a wheel.

The element that holds tension and creates connection. Not the centre. Not the edge. The connection between both, and the part that lets the system work as one.

We do not scale the person. We structure the system around her.

Kavitha Abzeena trained from childhood in Kerala. Her practice is never standardised, so the challenge is continuity: keeping that quality as it grows.

what it
became.

The KA · VI · THA system.

From the syllables of Kavitha's name, the ARA brand unfolds into four pillars, each with its own function and its own sub-brand.

The Center

ARA · KA

The point of diagnosis

The Medicine

ARA · VI

The point of formulation

The Experience

ARA · THA

The point of experience

The Institute

ARA · KAVITHA

The point of transmission

Then a three-step road to opening day.

  1. Brand foundation

    Identity, positioning, audiences, tone of voice and the visual system for the centres.

    4 months / Brand ID document

  2. Brand deployment

    Website, social, press and internal materials, plus a toolkit for every partner who carries ARA forward.

    6 months / Launch-ready presence

  3. Brand activation

    The full communication strategy, then the first campaign to bring the right people through the doors.

    6 months / Plan + first campaign

The knowledge exists. The practice exists. The people exist. ARA is the structure that lets them become one.

Sanjo

É um passo da tua história. A step in your story.

é um passo da tua história
Client
Sanjo
Sector
Footwear
What we did
Campaign concept, copy, packaging, film scripts, podcast, outdoor, retail and events
Format
Campaign proposal, with [in]sight studio

the
question?

How does a brand that is almost a century old talk to people who never lived its history?

The brand of the old days reinvented itself. So did its buyers: they shop online and meet brands there first.

Boomers & Gen X

Know the legacy and keep it alive. They also hold the buying power.

Millennials, Gen Z & Alpha

Nostalgic for things they never had.

The next generation

Ready to go past tradition altogether.

the
obvious
answer.

Tradition is passed from hand to hand.

the
gaffe.

Tradition is worn on your feet.

A tradição é uma herança passada entre mãos, mas que se calça nos pés.

the
insight.

Tudo pode mudar. Mas há coisas que caminham sempre connosco.

Everything can change. Some things always walk with us.

The young reach Sanjo through parents and grandparents. The legacy is real, but you wear it, and it takes you somewhere new.

what it
became.

Online and off

A um passo do online

One step from online

Packaging for online orders

A biscuit tin, with sneakers in it

The shoebox borrows from another piece of Portuguese memory: the blue metal biscuit tin that came from a grandparent's hands. Nostalgia is a card few brands can play. A brand this old can.

Two film spots

Herança & O Amanhã

A young woman scrolls through family photos and finds the same shoes in every decade. Then a black-and-white victory, and a sneaker thrown so high it keeps going.

Podcast

Um Par de Histórias

Real stories that began with a pair of shoes, told with special guests. Plus social formats, the brand site and retailer pages.

Com um pé no offline

One foot in offline

Fashion events

A photo studio shaped like a shoebox

Visitors pick an outfit outside, step into the box and leave with a professional portrait in Sanjo. The pictures go out on their own feeds.

Outdoor, radio, stores, retail

The same step, everywhere you walk

Online sales keep growing. The street still gives a brand what a screen cannot: something to stand in front of.

Não é um passo em falso. Não é um passo maior que a perna. É o próximo passo.

Not a false step. Not a step too far. The next step.
